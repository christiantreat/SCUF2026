Vitreous Hemorrhage

  • Echoes appear and multiply as gain increases
  • Particles float freely, not tethered to any structure
  • Swirl and shift when you sweep the probe
  • At low gain the vitreous looks falsely clear

Retinal Detachment

  • Bright membrane visible even at minimal gain
  • Anchored at the optic disc — classic tethered V
  • Stays fixed when you sweep; only the angle changes
  • Membrane brightness is gain-independent
